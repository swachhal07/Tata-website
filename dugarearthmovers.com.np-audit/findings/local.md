# Local SEO — dugarearthmovers.com.np

**Score:** 25/100  |  **Audit weight:** 0%  |  **Audited:** 14 August 2026

## What works

- 10 service and parts branches with city, coordinates, named contact and direct phone, admin-managed via locations.js.
- 7 additional named sales contacts across Biratnagar, Birgunj, Pokhara, Nepalgunj and Kathmandu.
- Interactive MapLibre map on /contact with Google Maps deep links per branch.
- tel: links used throughout - strong mobile conversion mechanics.

## Findings

### [High] No per-location pages

Ten branches, zero URLs to rank for '[service] [city]' queries.

**Fix:** Create /locations/:city pages with 300+ words of genuinely local content each.

### [High] NAP is incomplete and inconsistent

The footer gives only 'Kathmandu, Nepal' - no street address, no postal code, no opening hours anywhere on the site. locations.js has coordinates but no streetAddress field.

**Fix:** Collect and publish full street addresses and opening hours per branch; keep them identical across the site, GBP and directories.

### [Medium] Google Business Profile status not verifiable from the site

No GBP links and no review widgets pulling from Google. Local pack visibility for this business will come more from GBP than from the website.

**Fix:** Confirm all 10 branches have claimed, correctly-categorised GBP listings with matching NAP, photos and an active review-request habit.

### [Medium] Generic Gmail address as primary contact

sales.tatahitachinp@gmail.com is used sitewide on a business that owns its own domain - a weak trust signal for users and local ranking alike.

**Fix:** Move to sales@dugarearthmovers.com.np.
