# Search Experience (SXO) — dugarearthmovers.com.np

**Score:** 30/100  |  **Audit weight:** 0%  |  **Audited:** 14 August 2026

## What works

- Conversion mechanics are stronger than acquisition mechanics - tel: links throughout, named humans per branch, working enquiry form.
- Navigation is clear and every main section is reachable in one click.

## Findings

### [High] No above-the-fold value proposition

The hero is pure imagery with no text. A visitor arriving from search sees a video with no statement of who this is or what they sell until they scroll.

**Fix:** Add hero headline and subhead copy naming the brand, the distributor relationship and the coverage.

### [High] Page-type mismatch on /products

Commercial queries for a specific machine model expect a product detail page. A single category listing with anchors cannot satisfy that intent.

**Fix:** Individual product URLs - the highest-value content investment on the site.

### [Medium] Persona gap - the parts buyer

'Genuine Parts' is one homepage tile with no destination page, despite being a high-frequency, high-intent, repeat-purchase query cluster.

**Fix:** Build a dedicated genuine parts landing page.
