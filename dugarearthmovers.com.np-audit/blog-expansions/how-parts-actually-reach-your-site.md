A machine is only as good as the part you can get for it on a Tuesday afternoon. That is the whole reason our parts operation looks the way it does.

## Stocked, not ordered

The central warehouse carries the fast movers for every machine we have sold in Nepal — filters, seals, hoses, undercarriage components, common hydraulic parts. These are not ordered in when you call. They are on the shelf, and most of them leave the same day the order comes in.

Branch stores across our ten branches hold their own working stock on top of that, so a filter change in Nepalgunj does not wait on a dispatch from Kathmandu.

## What takes longer

Major structural components and less common assemblies are ordered against demand. Specialised parts are sourced direct from the factory, typically within 5–10 working days. We will tell you upfront when something falls into that category, and we will tell you the real date rather than an optimistic one.

## How to order so the right part arrives first time

Most delays are not about stock. They are about the order. A few details make the difference:

- The machine's model and serial number, from the plate on the frame. The same model can use different parts depending on its build.
- The part number from the old part or the parts book, if you have it.
- A photo of the part fitted on the machine, and of any number stamped on it.
- Where the machine is working, so we can send it from the nearest branch store.

With those four things, the parts desk can confirm availability and dispatch in the same call.

## What to keep on site

For a machine working away from a branch town, a small kit on site saves days. We usually suggest:

- One set of engine oil, fuel and air filters, so a scheduled service never waits on a delivery.
- A couple of spare bucket teeth and their pins.
- Grease, and the right grade of hydraulic and engine oil for top-ups.
- Two or three of the hoses that fail most often on your type of work.

Our parts team can put together a site kit for your machine and your application.

## Genuine parts, and why we push them

We understand the price argument. We also see what comes back through the workshop. A non-genuine hydraulic filter that lets fine particles through does not fail visibly — it quietly costs you a pump eighteen months later, and that bill is not close to the money saved.

The same pattern shows up with seals that harden early in the heat, hoses rated below the machine's working pressure, and undercarriage parts made from softer steel. They look identical on the shelf, and the difference only shows on the machine.

Every part we supply is covered by the manufacturer warranty, and every part fitted by our technicians is logged against the machine's service history. When you sell it, that history is worth real money.

## When the part needs a technician

Some parts are a ten-minute job for your own operator. Others — hydraulic pumps, final drives, electronic controllers — need the right tools and the right procedure. Our factory-trained technicians can typically be on site within 24 to 48 hours from any of our ten branches, carrying the parts with them.

Call your nearest branch from the contact page, or tell us the machine and the part and we will check stock while you are on the line.
